1. Executive Summary
The Establishment Module of the Maryland Child Support Management System (CSMS) manages the complete legal and administrative process of establishing paternity and a child support order. It is triggered immediately after the Case Management module has completed the Custodial Party (CP), Non-Custodial Parent (NCP), and Child profiles.
The module encompasses eight functional screens, coordinated across three primary roles — IV-D Attorney, Case Worker, and Supervisor — plus automated system calculations. Upon successful completion, the case transfers automatically to the Enforcement Module.

1.1 Module Trigger & Handoff
TRIGGER: Establishment Module activates when Case Management marks the case as 'Profiles Complete' and routes to the Establishment queue. INPUT: Verified CP profile, NCP profile (located or pending locate), and children profiles. OUTPUT: Active court-ordered child support obligation, IWO issued, case transferred to Enforcement.


1.2 Module Objectives
Establish legal paternity when not already confirmed
Locate the Non-Custodial Parent if address or employment is unknown
Collect verified financial information from both parties
Calculate the child support obligation per Maryland Income Shares Guidelines
Obtain and record a legal court order
Issue the Income Withholding Order (IWO) to the NCP employer
Complete service of process on the NCP
Obtain supervisor sign-off and transfer the case to Enforcement

3. End-to-End Workflow Narrative
The following describes the complete sequential flow of the Establishment Module. Each phase references a screen, its responsible role, decision points, and the data produced.

PHASE 1 — PATERNITY CHECK   (Screen 1 · IV-D Attorney)


Entry Point: The IV-D Attorney receives the case from Case Management. The first task is to determine whether legal paternity has already been established.

Decision: Is Paternity Already Established?
YES → Skip genetic testing, record the establishment method (prior court order, marriage presumption, or existing AOP) and proceed to Financial Info (Screen 3).
NO → Proceed with one of the three establishment pathways below.

Pathway A — Voluntary Acknowledgment of Paternity (AOP)
Both parents sign a Voluntary AOP form at a hospital, LBVS office, or CSEA office. The 60-day rescission window must expire before the AOP is legally binding. Once rescission expires, paternity is established and the case proceeds.

Pathway B — Genetic Testing
When paternity is contested or an AOP is unavailable, a court orders genetic testing. The attorney coordinates with a certified laboratory (e.g., LabCorp, Quest). Samples are collected from the CP, NCP, and child with verified chain of custody. Maryland's legal threshold is a probability of paternity of 99% or greater. A positive result (>=99%) confirms the NCP as the legal father.

Pathway C — Default Judgment
If the NCP refuses genetic testing or fails to appear after service of process, the court may enter a default judgment establishing paternity. The attorney documents the refusal and the default basis.

DATA OUTPUT FROM SCREEN 1: Paternity Established flag, Establishment Method, Legal Father Name, Establishment Date, Lab Report Document ID (if DNA), assigned IV-D Attorney.


PHASE 2 — LOCATE PARENT   (Screen 2 · Case Worker · CONDITIONAL)


This screen is ONLY triggered when the NCP's current address or employment is unknown. If the NCP was located during Case Management, this phase is skipped entirely.

Locate Sources Used (in priority order)
National Directory of New Hires (NDNH) — employer and wage data from new hire reporting
Maryland DMV Records — current address from driver's license / vehicle registration
State Verification and Exchange System (SVES / SSA) — disability / SSI benefits
IRS Locate — tax return address data (requires IRS data match agreement)
Maryland State Wage Match — quarterly wage records from MD DLLR
Department of Corrections (DOC) — incarceration records

Once the NCP is located, the confirmed address and employer are saved, and the workflow returns to Screen 1 (Paternity Check) if not yet complete, or proceeds to Screen 3 (Financial Info) if paternity is already established.
DATA OUTPUT FROM SCREEN 2: Confirmed NCP Address, Address Source (NDNH/DMV/etc.), Date Confirmed, Confirmed Employer Name, Employer EIN, Locate Status (Located / Not Located).

PHASE 5 — SUPPORT OBLIGATION CALCULATION   (Screen 5 · System Auto)


The system automatically calculates the child support obligation using the Maryland Income Shares Model (FL §12-201). This model determines both parties' proportional share of the total obligation based on their combined income.

Optional Adjustments
Parenting Time Adjustment: Applied if NCP has 128 or more overnight visits per year. Reduces NCP obligation proportionally.
Deviation: Attorney or parties may request a deviation from guidelines. Requires written justification and judicial approval.
Retroactive Support: System calculates arrears from a specified start date (typically child's date of birth or date of separation).
DATA OUTPUT FROM SCREEN 5: Proposed Monthly Support Amount ($931.00), Retroactive Arrears Amount ($2,793.00), Medical Support obligation, Parenting time adjustment (if any), Deviation justification (if any).


PHASE 6 — ORDER ESTABLISHMENT   (Screen 6 · Attorney + Court)


The attorney prepares and submits the child support order to the Circuit Court. Three order types are possible based on the parties' responses.

Order Types
Order Type
When Used and Process
Consent Order
Both parties agree to the calculated amount. Attorney prepares order; both parties sign; judge approves. Fastest path.
Default Order
NCP fails to respond after being served. Court enters order after response deadline (30 days). NCP's failure to respond is documented.
Contested Order
NCP disputes the order. Case goes to a formal court hearing before a judge or hearing officer. Can add weeks to the timeline.
Administrative Order
CSEA administrative process; used in qualifying IV-D cases. Follows COMAR 07.07.03 administrative hearing procedures.


Order Contents Recorded in CSMS
Court docket/order number, order date, effective date
Monthly child support amount ($931.00)
Payment due date and frequency (1st of month, monthly)
Retroactive/arrears amount and payment schedule
Health insurance responsibility (CP or NCP) and deadline
Unreimbursed medical expense split (default 50/50)
Emancipation conditions

Income Withholding Order (IWO)
An IWO is mandatory in Maryland under FL §10-120. It is issued to the NCP's employer on the same date the support order is entered. The employer is required to withhold and remit payment to the Maryland State Disbursement Unit (SDU).

DATA OUTPUT FROM SCREEN 6: Court Order Number, Order Type, Effective Date, Monthly Obligation ($931), IWO Amount ($1,031), SDU Account Number, Employer EIN.


PHASE 7 — SERVICE OF PROCESS   (Screen 7 · IV-D Attorney / Process Server)


The NCP must be legally served with the court documents (Summons, Complaint, or Petition). Each service attempt is logged in CSMS. The NCP has 30 days from confirmed service to respond.

Service Methods (in order of preference)
Personal Service — Sheriff's deputy or licensed process server physically delivers documents to NCP
Certified Mail — Documents mailed to NCP's confirmed address with return receipt
Substituted Service — Documents left with a responsible adult at NCP's residence
Posting — Documents posted at NCP's last known address (requires court approval)
Publication — Published in a newspaper of general circulation (requires judge's order after failed attempts)

Service Decision Flow
Attempt 1: Personal service at confirmed address
Attempt 2: If failed — certified mail or substituted service
Attempt 3+: Attorney files motion for alternative service (publication/posting)
If NCP evades service: Attorney documents and court may enter default judgment

NCP Response After Service
NCP Response
Next Action
Answer Filed — Consent
Attorney prepares consent order; proceed to Case Review
No Response (Default)
Default judgment entered after 30-day window; proceed to Case Review
Contest Filed
Case scheduled for court hearing; contested order process begins
DATA OUTPUT FROM SCREEN 7: Service method used, Date served, Process server name, Affidavit of Service document ID, NCP Response type, NCP Response Deadline date, Default Judgment Eligible flag.

4.1 Key Data Dependencies
Screen 5 (Calculation) REQUIRES both Screen 3 and Screen 4 to be COMPLETE before it can execute. The system will block the attorney from submitting Screen 5 until both financial screens are saved and validated.


4.2 Federal Reporting Data Flows
Federal Case Registry (FCR): Updated at order establishment (Screen 6) with case number, order date, and NCP SSN.
National Directory of New Hires (NDNH): Queried in Screen 2 for locate; also updated when NCP employment changes.
State Case Registry (SCR): Updated at case finalization (Screen 8) with full order details.
State Disbursement Unit (SDU): Receives IWO details from Screen 6 and begins accepting payments from employer.
