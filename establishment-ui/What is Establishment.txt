What is Establishment?

Establishment is the part of the system that helps:

Figure out who the dad is (paternity)

Decide how much child support to pay (support order)
Save that in an official court order
Simple Story Flow

1. Mom asks for help


Mom comes to the child support office.

She says: “I need help getting support from my child’s dad.”

2. The office finds the dad (Locate)

Staff search for the dad (address, employer, etc.).

3. Establishment starts

If we don’t know who the dad is → Paternity (AOP or genetic test).

If we know who the dad is → We move to creating the support order.

4. Court makes the order

The court decides:

How much the dad pays each month

When payments start

Any arrears (past-due support)

This is written down in a court order.

5. Everything is saved in the system

The system stores:

Court order type and dates
Obligation (how much, how often)
Arrears
Medical support
Wage withholding (employer info)
Intergovernmental info (if another state is involved)
