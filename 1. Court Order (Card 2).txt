1. Court Order (Card 2)
Purpose: Records the basic details of the court order that creates the child support obligation.

Used when: A judge signs a new or modified support order.

What it captures:
Court Order Type – What kind of order (e.g., Paternity, Support, Modification).
Court Order Date – When the order was signed.

Court Order Process – Which process produced the order.

Date of Stay – When the order was temporarily stopped (if applicable).
Reason for Stay – Why the order was stayed.
Bond – Any bond (amount and date) tied to the case.
Regenerate missed accounts – Whether to rebuild accounts that were missed.
Obligation Type – Type of support (Current, Arrears Only, etc.).
Court Order Entry Date – When the order was entered into the system.
Controlling Order Jurisdiction – Which state/court controls the order.
Reason for Judicial Termination – Why the order was ended (if terminated).

2. Support Obligation Information (Card 3)
Purpose: Captures how the support amount was calculated and arrears details.
Used when: The court sets ongoing support and/or arrears obligations.
What it captures:
Guidelines Template/Amount – Which guidelines were used and the calculated amount.
Deviation Amount/Date/Reason – Any adjustment from the guideline amount and why.
Reset Compliance Balance – Whether to reset payment compliance tracking.
Include Arrears / Arrears Only / Arrears SOA – Whether past‑due support is included and how.
Arrears Frequency, Effective/Expiration/Balance as of Date – How often arrears are paid and the balance as of a given date.

Future Arrears SOA – Future arrears support obligation amount and its schedule.

Converted Arrears – Arrears that were converted to another type of obligation.
Comments – Additional notes.

3. Medical Support (Card 4)
Purpose: Records who must provide health coverage and related medical support.
Used when: The court orders health insurance or other medical support.
What it captures:
Medical Support Order – Type of order (e.g., employer coverage, cash medical).
Obligated Party – Who must provide coverage (usually NCP).
Medical Support Order Date – When the order was issued.
Medical Support Provided – Whether and how coverage is provided.
Medical Support Provided Date – When coverage began.
Include Arrears – Whether to include past‑due medical support.
4. Wage Withholding (Card 5)
Purpose: Records how support will be collected from the NCP’s paycheck (IWO/EWO).
Used when: Setting up or changing wage withholding for a support order.
What it captures:
Wage Withholding – Whether and how wage withholding applies.
Wage Withholding Date – When withholding takes effect.
Employer EWO Exclusion – Whether an employer is excluded from electronic wage withholding.
Employer Exclusion Date – When that exclusion took effect.
Employer EWO Exclusion Reason – Why the employer is excluded.
5. Judgement (Card 6)
Purpose: Records money judgments (e.g., for arrears or other amounts owed).
Used when: The court enters a judgment for a specific amount.
What it captures:
Judgement Amount – Amount of the judgment.
Judgement Effective/Expiration Date – When it starts and ends.
Judgement Satisfied Date – When it was fully paid.
Future SOA – Future support obligation amount, frequency, and dates.
Include Arrears – Whether arrears are part of the judgment.
6. Intergovernmental (Card 7)
Purpose: Records when the case or order involves another state, county, or country.
Used when: The case is interstate or international (UIFSA).
What it captures:
State – Other state(s) involved.
County – Other county involved.
International – Country involved (if applicable).
Non‑MD Case Number – Case number from the other jurisdiction.
Non‑MD Court Order – Court order from the other jurisdiction.
7. Intergovernmental Tax (Card 8)
Purpose: Records tax‑related handling when another country is involved.
Used when: Investigating or using foreign tax offsets (e.g., treaty countries).
What it captures:
Foreign Tax Offset Investigation Request Date – When the investigation was requested.
Investigation Disposition – Result (e.g., Approved, Denied).
Investigation Disposition Date – When that result was set.
Interest – Any interest related to the tax offset.
Penalties – Any penalties related to the tax offset.
8. Review Information (Card 9)
Purpose: Tracks required and completed reviews of the support order.
Used when: Scheduling or completing triennial or other reviews of the order.
What it captures:
Next Triennial Review Date – When the next three‑year review is due.
Preliminary Review Requested/Decision Date – When it was requested and decided.
Preliminary Review Status – Current status of the preliminary review.
Final Review Date – When the final review was done.
Final Review Status – Outcome of the final review.
Last Triennial Review Notice Sent – When the last triennial notice was mailed.
9. Adjustment (Card 10)
Purpose: Tracks requests to change the order and the court’s decision.
Used when: Someone asks for a modification or adjustment.
What it captures:
Requesting Party – Who requested the change (CP, NCP, Agency, etc.).
Request Type – Type of change requested.
Judicial Disposition – Court’s decision (Granted, Denied, etc.).
Also related: Hearing Date, Date of Modified Order, Requested Documentation.
